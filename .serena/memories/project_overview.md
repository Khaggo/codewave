AUTOCARE is a Windows monorepo for Cruisers Crib Auto Care Center. Runtime applications are `backend/apps/main-service` (NestJS, port 3000), `frontend/` (Next.js staff and super-admin portal, port 3002), and `mobile/` (Expo customer app, Metro 8081 and optional web 8090). PostgreSQL is the system of record, Drizzle is the ORM, and REST/OpenAPI is the synchronous contract. Canonical architecture truth lives under `docs/architecture`; engineering status and QA evidence live under `docs/project-control`; execution tasks live under `docs/architecture/tasks`; integration contracts live under `docs/contracts`. Staff web is limited to service advisers and super admins. Technicians are non-login operational profiles. The customer app owns booking, garage, insurance, notifications, service tracking, and service rewards.
